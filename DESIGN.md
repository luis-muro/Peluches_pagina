## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, tactile, and premium boutique experience centered on iconic character heritage. It balances whimsical nostalgia with modern editorial restraint, evoking the timeless comfort of mid-century comics alongside the tactile luxury of heirloom-grade plush collectibles. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Modern-Boutique: generous whitespace, organic pill shapes, warm cream paper backdrops, and ink-line accents reminiscent of original comic strips. Visuals reject cold corporate gloss in favor of velvety drop shadows, hand-touched details, and cheerful character micro-moments. The interface targets adult collectors, thoughtful gift-givers, and dedicated Peanuts enthusiasts seeking authentic, heirloom-quality craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from Charles M. Schulz’s iconic color universe, calibrated for contemporary digital luxury:

- **Primary (`#F9C631` - Charlie Brown Gold)**: Used for primary calls-to-action, high-visibility badges, celebratory banners, and focus rings. Radiates warmth and instant recognition.
- **Secondary (`#E84135` - Doghouse Vermilion)**: Reserved for urgent action prompts, sale/edition tags, heart favoriting states, and special promotional callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#89C2D9` - Sky Blue)**: Evokes the open comic sky; utilized for seasonal collections, informational pill tags, packaging badges, and subtle tonal backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#1E1E24` - Comic Ink Charcoal)**: A softened, ultra-deep charcoal serving as the anchor for primary typography, borders, and high-contrast structural accents instead of harsh `#000000`.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiers**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Off-White Cream)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White)
  - `surface-muted`: `#F2EDE4` (Warm Oatmeal)

## Typography

The type system blends structural character with soft readability:
- **Headlines (Epilogue)**: Imparts a bold, graphic, slightly mid-century editorial structure. High weights (700/800) create cheerful punchiness reminiscent of classic comic book titles, while preserving sophisticated modern proportions.
- **Body & Labels (Nunito Sans)**: Offers gentle, rounded letter terminals that deliver maximum legibility without appearing juvenile. At small label sizes, letter-spacing is slightly tracked out for optical clarity on product specifications and tactile pill labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid within a responsive 12-column layout container (max width: 1280px):
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px page margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) page margins.

Rhythm is airy and deliberate. Products are framed with generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to mimic museum plinths or boutique vitrines. Sections utilize alternating cream and oatmeal bands with 80px to 120px vertical margins to distinguish between storytelling, collection showcases, and artisanal guarantees.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, plush-like tactile shadows combined with crisp, low-contrast ink outlines:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base)**: `#FAF7F2` flat background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Interactive Cards & Drawers)**: `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle border of `1.5px solid rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.08)` and an ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals & Flyout Cart)**: `#FFFFFF` with `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.05)`.
- **Physical "Squish" States**: Buttons and interactive tiles employ micro-elevations that sink 2px downward on `:active`, softening the shadow to simulate physical plush compression.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary prioritizes organic, friendly contours:
- Full pill radii are utilized for action buttons, filter tags, badges, and trust indicators.
- Cards, containers, and product imagery frames utilize heavy rounded corners (`rounded-lg` at `2rem` / 32px), creating a cozy, safe, and huggable physical presence.
- Badges and stamps may feature occasional gentle scallop or speech-bubble flourishes to echo comic heritage.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid Charlie Brown Gold (`#F9C631`), Comic Ink text (`#1E1E24`), font `label-lg`, full pill radius (`9999px`), 16px vertical by 28px horizontal padding. Subtle dark-rim tactile border `1.5px solid #1E1E24`. Hover shifts shadow deeper; active translates down `2px`.
- **Secondary / Cart Accent**: Doghouse Vermilion (`#E84135`) background with crisp white text for "Adopt Now" or checkout actions.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent surface, border `1.5px solid #1E1E24`, ink text, pill radius.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status & Editions**: Full pill shape, uppercase `label-sm`. 
  - "Collector's Edition": Cream background with `#E84135` text and outline.
  - "Handcrafted / Limited": Sky Blue (`#89C2D9`) fill with `#1E1E24` text.
- **Filter Chips**: Muted cream pill (`#F2EDE4`), transitioning to Ink (`#1E1E24`) background with pure white text when selected.

### Product Cards
- Pure white container with `rounded-lg` (32px radius) and 1.5px outline in `rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.08)`.
- Upper photo frame set against a subtle circular Sky Blue or Golden accent backdrop.
- Heart/Favorite icon button pinned to top-right (pill-circular, white surface, tactile drop shadow).
- Bottom area: `headline-sm` title, soft `body-sm` plush dimensions (e.g., "12-inch Huggable Scale"), price in bold charcoal, and full-width pill "Add to Bag" button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1.5px solid rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.16)`.
- Radius: `rounded-lg` (16px).
- Focus state: Border color transitions to `#F9C631` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(249, 198, 49, 0.3)` glow.
- Checkboxes and Radios: Pill-soft rounded corners, heavy check glyphs, filled with `#1E1E24` when checked.

### Trust Icons & Artisanal Badges
- Soft circular containers (`#F2EDE4` or `#89C2D9` at 20% opacity) housing simplified black-line illustrations (e.g., "Officially Licensed Peanuts", "Ultra-Soft Hypoallergenic Plush", "Eco-Conscious Stitching").
